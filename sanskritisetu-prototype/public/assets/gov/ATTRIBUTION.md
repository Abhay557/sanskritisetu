# Government asset attribution (prototype demo use)

All files in this folder are used for the SIH 2026 prototype only.

1. `emblem-india.svg` — State Emblem of India (Lion Capital of Ashoka, motto सत्यमेव जयते).
   Work of the Government of India (sourced from legislative.gov.in, A2005-50).
   Public Domain in India (PD-India). Wikimedia Commons mirror used as download
   convenience; insignia use restrictions apply. Links: https://www.presentations.gov.in/logos/national-emblem

2. `flag-india.svg` — Flag of India (Tiranga, 3:2 with Navy Blue Ashoka Chakra).
   Public-domain construction. Respect the Flag Code of India, 2002 and the
   Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971. No distortion, no text over it.

3. `pm-modi-official.jpg` — "Official Photograph of Prime Minister Narendra Modi".
   Source: https://pmindia.gov.in/en/image-gallery (PMO, content managed by Prime
   Minister's Office, hosted by NIC). Used with on-screen caption + link back to
   https://www.pmindia.gov.in. Do not crop out dignity, do not overlay text on face.

General: data directory sourced from https://www.india.gov.in/explore-india/travel-and-tourism
(National Portal of India; content owned by respective Ministries/Departments, district websites).
